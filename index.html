About the Internship Project

This internship project has been developed under the Department of Political Science as part of academic internship activities. Political Science is one of the most important social sciences which studies the structure and functioning of the state, government, constitution, democracy, public administration, political behavior, citizenship, rights, duties, justice, equality, liberty, and international relations.

The main objective of this internship project is to create awareness about political institutions, democratic values, leadership, governance, constitutional principles, and responsible citizenship. Through this internship, an attempt has been made to understand the relationship between citizens and the state along with the importance of participation in democratic processes.

Political Science helps students understand the functioning of Parliament, Judiciary, Executive, Election Commission, political parties, public policies, and international organizations. It also develops critical thinking, leadership qualities, social responsibility, decision-making ability, and awareness regarding national and global political issues.

This internship project also highlights the importance of education in strengthening democracy and social harmony. It encourages students to become responsible citizens who understand constitutional values, secularism, justice, equality, human rights, and the importance of peaceful coexistence in society.

The internship work further focuses on developing practical, organizational, communication, and analytical skills among students. It also reflects the significance of discipline, cooperation, teamwork, leadership, and active participation in educational and social activities.

Nazir Ajmal Memorial College of Education, affiliated to Gauhati University, plays an important role in promoting higher education, value-based learning, academic excellence, and personality development among students. This internship project has been prepared as part of the educational and practical learning process under the FYUGP 5th Semester Political Science Department.

Objectives of the Internship

Political Awareness

To create awareness among students regarding democracy, citizenship, constitution, elections, governance, and political participation.

Leadership Development

To develop leadership qualities, communication skills, social responsibility, and decision-making ability among students.

Understanding Democracy

To understand the functioning of Parliament, Judiciary, Executive, Election Commission, and democratic institutions.

Social Responsibility

To encourage students to become responsible citizens who respect justice, equality, liberty, secularism, and human rights.

Importance of Political Science

Political Science plays an important role in understanding society and governance. It helps students understand how laws are made, how governments function, and how citizens participate in democratic systems.

It also explains the importance of justice, rights, equality, secularism, constitutional values, and international peace. Political Science develops awareness about current political issues, public administration, foreign policy, and global relations.

The subject also develops analytical thinking, leadership qualities, social ethics, public speaking, and problem-solving ability among students. It prepares students for responsible participation in social and political life.
