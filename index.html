About the Internship

Internship is an important part of academic and practical learning. It provides students with opportunities to gain practical experience, improve communication skills, develop leadership qualities, and understand professional environments in a better way. Internship programs help students connect classroom learning with practical implementation and real-life activities.

This internship project has been developed under the Political Science Department. Political Science is one of the most important social science subjects which studies government, constitution, democracy, citizenship, public administration, justice, equality, liberty, political institutions, international relations, and public policies.

The subject helps students understand the functioning of Parliament, Judiciary, Executive, Election Commission, political parties, democratic institutions, and the relationship between citizens and the state. Political Science also develops awareness regarding constitutional values, human rights, secularism, governance, and social responsibility.

Through this internship project, an attempt has been made to create awareness regarding democratic values, citizenship education, social harmony, discipline, cooperation, leadership, and active participation in educational and social activities.

Internship activities also help students improve organizational skills, communication ability, teamwork, analytical thinking, problem-solving skills, and confidence. It encourages students to become responsible citizens who contribute positively to society and nation-building.

Nazir Ajmal Memorial College of Education, affiliated to Gauhati University, plays an important role in promoting educational excellence, value-based learning, social ethics, personality development, and practical education among students.

This internship project has been prepared as part of the FYUGP 5th Semester academic activities under the Political Science Department. The project reflects the importance of practical learning, discipline, responsibility, and educational development.

Objectives of the Internship

Political Awareness

To create awareness among students regarding democracy, citizenship, governance, constitution, elections, and political participation.

Leadership Development

To improve leadership qualities, communication skills, teamwork, and decision-making ability among students.

Social Responsibility

To encourage students to become disciplined, responsible, ethical, and socially aware citizens.

Educational Growth

To connect theoretical knowledge with practical learning experiences and educational activities.

Importance of Political Science

Political Science plays a significant role in understanding society, governance, constitutional systems, public administration, law-making processes, and democratic institutions.

It develops awareness regarding justice, equality, liberty, human rights, secularism, and peaceful coexistence. The subject also explains the importance of active citizenship and participation in democratic systems.

Political Science encourages analytical thinking, critical understanding, leadership qualities, public speaking skills, social ethics, and awareness regarding national and global issues.

The subject also helps students understand international organizations, foreign policies, global relations, political ideologies, and the importance of maintaining peace, harmony, and cooperation among nations.

Internship Activities

The internship activities included participation in educational programs, communication activities, classroom interaction, awareness programs, leadership development exercises, teamwork activities, and organizational responsibilities.

Students also participated in academic discussions, practical learning sessions, social awareness activities, and educational observation programs related to Political Science and social studies.

Internship activities helped students understand the importance of discipline, punctuality, communication, cooperation, confidence, and educational management.
